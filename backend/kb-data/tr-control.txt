Portfolio passage ID: tr-control

Project: TraffiX

Passage: One writer per light, a reason for every decision

Context: Architecture made by our team of six.

Verified notes: Only one controller per traffic light may change it, a supermaster coordinates the junctions, and every decision is logged with a reason code. A language layer, Synapse, explains decisions from that log and never acts on the simulation.

Limitations:
- The agent architecture was made by our team of six, not by me alone.
