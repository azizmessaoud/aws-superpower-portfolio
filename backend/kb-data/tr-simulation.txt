Portfolio passage ID: tr-simulation

Project: TraffiX

Passage: A simulation, not a deployment

Context: ESPRIT 4DS course project, Intelligent Multi-Agent Traffic Management (team of six).

Verified notes: TraffiX is a multi-agent traffic-control simulation of six signalised junctions (J1–J6) on the real OpenStreetMap streets of central Tunis, built on Eclipse SUMO and driven through TraCI. It is not deployed on any street: road demand is synthetic and nothing in the project is deployment evidence.

Limitations:
- Simulation only. Road demand is synthetic; emissions are proxies, not measured air quality.
