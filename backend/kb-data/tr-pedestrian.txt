Passage: Fewer car greens while people cross

Context: Tunis map, mean of seeds 1–3, three scenarios, models and network of 20 October 2026.

Verified notes: With the trained models, about 18 car green phases per run started while pedestrians were crossing, against 331–405 with SUMO's actuated signals (−95%). The number is not zero.

Limitations:
- Three seeds and three scenarios; no difference is significant at p < 0.05.
- Simulated with synthetic demand; not a safety result for real streets.
