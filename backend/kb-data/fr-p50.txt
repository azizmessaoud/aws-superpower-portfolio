Project: FlyRank capstone

Passage: Precision@50 of 0.94 against 0.76

Context: Features from 31 March 2026; outcome = Google Search Console clicks 1–7 April 2026.

Verified notes: On 1,583 held-out pages from 8 clients the model never saw, a Random Forest put higher-traffic pages in its top 50 with Precision@50 of 0.94, against 0.76 for the fixed baseline. A hit is a page with at least 8 clicks in the following week, the 75th percentile of the training rows.

Limitations:
- One decision date and one outcome week; observational, not causal.
- The internship warehouse is gated, so the result cannot be fully reproduced in public.
