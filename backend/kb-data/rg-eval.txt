Passage: How the administrative procedures assistant's retrieval was evaluated

Context: A college team project. I worked on the retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) component, which is my own account; these are the team's reported results, from its evaluation of 16 Oct 2026.

Verified notes: In the team's multilingual assistant for Tunisian administrative procedures, hybrid retrieval (dense search, BM25 and rank fusion, with at most two chunks per document) found a correct passage in the top 5 for 0.873 of 71 labelled development questions, against 0.732 for dense search alone. On 20 held-out questions written after the tuning it scored 0.750, the same as dense search, and ranked the first correct passage lower (MRR@5 0.531 against 0.635). The team's report says part of the development gain was overfitting.

Limitations:
- The team's own numbers; this site has not re-run them.
- The held-out set is small (20 questions), and the term maps were built after looking at failures on the development set.
- No evaluation label has been reviewed by a person yet; the team's report marks every item's review as pending.
- When the report was written, the running backend still served the earlier code, so this is not a claim about a deployed system.
